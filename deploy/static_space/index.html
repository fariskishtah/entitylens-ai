About

EntityLens AI recognizes four entity types — Person, Organization, Location and Miscellaneous — with a bert-base-cased model fine-tuned on CoNLL-2003 (test F1 0.918). This demo runs the quantized model entirely in your browser via transformers.js — no data leaves your device. Highlighting uses exact character offsets from the tokenizer.

Model: huggingface.co/fariskishtah/entitylens-ai · Code: github.com/fariskishtah/entitylens-ai
